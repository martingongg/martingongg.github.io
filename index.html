    05 — Interactive lab

    Live simulation

    Architecture is easier to see when you can run it.

    Compare round-robin against instruction-ID-aware MMU arbitration, or switch tabs to place standard cells across power rails.

    +20.4%
    Arbitration policy
    ID-aware crossbar
    Queue behavior
    Out-of-order bypass

    Under round-robin, slow page-table walks stall unrelated translations behind them. Tracking instruction IDs lets independent hits bypass the walk queue.
