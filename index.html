Hi, I’m Qijun 👋 — I design hardware

I’m a fourth-year ECE student at the University of Washington (B.S., June 2027) focused on RTL, digital design, verification, and FPGAs.

At StarFive IP (Singapore, Summer 2026) I worked on CPU memory hardware: I first measured a round-robin DTLB prefetch scheme and found it barely beat no prefetch at all — so I scrapped it and built an IID-recognizing arbiter from scratch that met the team’s 20% MMU speedup demand. That loop — measure, be willing to throw work away, rebuild on evidence — is how I like to engineer.

What I’m working on

  • CPU / MMU microarchitecture — arbitration, prefetch, translation hardware
  • Standard cells — INVD1 schematic, layout, DRC / LVS / PEX in Cadence on FreePDK (EE 476)
  • Power & avionics hardware — 400V systems on the PACCAR E-Truck, ESP32 flight tracking, AIAA Design Build Fly (3rd worldwide)
  • Research direction — PNCEL, CacheFlex, turning cache ways into software-managed scratchpad for wide vector / SIMD
